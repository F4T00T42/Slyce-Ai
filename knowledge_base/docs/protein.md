---
title: Dietary Protein
source: https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov / https://ods.od.nih.gov
---
Protein is one of the three macronutrients, alongside carbohydrate and fat. It
provides about 4 calories per gram and supplies amino acids used to build and
repair muscle, enzymes, hormones, and other tissues.

The Recommended Dietary Allowance (RDA) for protein is about 0.8 grams per
kilogram of body weight per day for the average sedentary adult. People who are
physically active, building muscle, or losing weight often benefit from higher
intakes, commonly in the range of 1.2 to 2.2 grams per kilogram per day.
Spreading protein across meals (roughly 20-40 grams per meal) supports muscle
protein synthesis better than consuming it all at once.

Good sources include poultry, fish, eggs, dairy, legumes, tofu, and lean meats.
Plant-based eaters can meet needs by combining a variety of legumes, grains, and
seeds across the day.
