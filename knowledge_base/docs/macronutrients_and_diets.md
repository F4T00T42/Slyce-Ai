---
title: Macronutrients and Common Diet Patterns
source: https://www.who.int / https://www.eatright.org
---
The three macronutrients are carbohydrate (4 kcal/g), protein (4 kcal/g), and
fat (9 kcal/g). General healthy-eating guidance suggests roughly 45-65% of
calories from carbohydrate, 20-35% from fat, and 10-35% from protein, though
individual targets vary by goal.

Ketogenic (keto) diets are very low in carbohydrate (often under 5-10% of
calories) and high in fat (around 65-75%), shifting the body toward using fat
and ketones for fuel. High-protein diets emphasize 30% or more of calories from
protein and are often used to support satiety and muscle retention during fat
loss. Balanced diets keep all three macronutrients within moderate ranges.

Fiber, found in vegetables, fruits, legumes, and whole grains, supports
digestion and satiety; many guidelines recommend about 25-38 grams per day.
Sodium intake is commonly recommended to stay under about 2,300 mg per day.
